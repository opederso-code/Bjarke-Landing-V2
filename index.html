Bjärke

Safety that doesn’t look like safety. Jewelry that pays attention when you can’t, and calls for backup when it counts.

Bjärke is an AI-powered pendant that lets you worry less and enjoy more: the walk home, the solo Uber to the party, the conversation that starts to feel wrong.

A discreet built-in camera reads what’s happening around you. When something feels off, Bjärke notices first. It records everything as evidence and, if it’s warranted, alerts your emergency contact.

And if you need it, a bright light flashes to tell anyone nearby to back off. The rest of the time, it’s chic enough to wear anywhere the night takes you.
