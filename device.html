How It Works

Bjärke’s AI hardware is smaller than a fingertip, so it fits inside the shell you choose. Swap the look whenever you like. The protection stays the same.

A tiny camera keeps an eye on what’s around you while you enjoy your night.

02 — Notices

Built-in AI knows what a normal night looks like, so it can tell when something isn’t right.

03 — Records & alerts

If something’s wrong, Bjärke starts recording and lets your emergency contact know where you are.

04 — Lights up

A bright flash tells anyone nearby to back off.
